## Summary
**MRI Safety Lecture Notes Summary**

**Main Topics:**

1. **Static Magnetic Field Hazards**: A magnetic field can pose a risk to patients with certain pacemakers, as they can become projectiles.
2. **Gradient Field Risks**: Gradient fields can cause heating, which can lead to tissue damage.
3. **RF Heating and SAR**: Radiofrequency (RF) heating can cause tissue damage, and the Surface Area Radiation (SAR) limit protects against this.
4. **Contraindications for Implants**: Certain implants, such as pacemakers, are contraindicated for MRI scanning due to the risk of static magnetic field hazards.

**Key Safety Points:**

- Patients with pacemakers must not enter the MRI suite.
- Ferromagnetic objects can become projectiles in the magnetic field.
- The SAR limit protects against tissue heating.
- It is essential to verify implant compatibility before scanning to avoid potential hazards.
