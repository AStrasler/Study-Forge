## Summary
**MRI Safety Lecture Notes Summary**

**Main Topics:**

1. **Static Magnetic Field Hazards**: The MRI scanner's static magnetic field can pose a risk to patients with certain medical implants, such as pacemakers.
2. **Gradient Field Risks**: Gradient fields can also pose a risk, particularly for patients with ferromagnetic implants or other metal objects.
3. **RF Heating and SAR (Superficial Anticancer Radiation)**: The RF field used in MRI can cause heating and potential damage to the surrounding tissues, known as SAR.
4. **Contraindications for Implants**: Certain implants, including pacemakers, are contraindicated for MRI scanning due to the potential risks mentioned above.

**Key Safety Points:**

- Patients with pacemakers are advised not to enter the MRI suite.
- Ferromagnetic objects, such as metal prosthetics or implants, can become projectiles in the magnetic field and pose a risk.
- SAR limits protect against tissue heating.
- Verifying implant compatibility before scanning is crucial to minimize risks.

**Professor's Tip:**

- Always verify implant compatibility before performing an MRI scan.
