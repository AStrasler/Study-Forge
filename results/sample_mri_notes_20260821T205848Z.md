## Summary
**MRI Safety Lecture Notes Summary**

**Main Topics:**

1. **Static Magnetic Field Hazards**: A static magnetic field can pose a risk to patients with certain pacemakers, as they can interfere with the device's functioning.
2. **Gradient Field Risks**: Gradient fields, which are used to produce high-resolution images, can cause heating and potential tissue damage.
3. **RF Heating and SAR (Sarcoma Risk Assessment)**: Radiofrequency (RF) heating can cause damage to tissues, particularly in the presence of certain implants.
4. **Contraindications for Implants**: Patients with metal implants, such as pacemakers, are generally not eligible for MRI scans due to the risks associated with the magnetic field and RF radiation.

**Key Safety Points:**

- Patients with pacemakers must avoid the MRI suite.
- Ferromagnetic objects can become projectiles in the magnetic field.
- SAR limits protect against tissue heating.

**Professor's Tip:**

- Verify implant compatibility before scanning to minimize risks.
